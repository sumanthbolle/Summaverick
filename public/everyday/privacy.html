Summaverick Everyday · Privacy

Your recordings stay on your iPhone.

Summaverick Everyday has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Summaverick Everyday is made by SUMMAVERICK LLP (Summaverick Group). This policy covers the Summaverick Everyday app for iPhone. Questions: hello@summaverick.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or sell any data about you, and we do not track you across apps or websites. There is no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting service and no advertising code in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Recordings, transcripts, notes, the answers Ask gives you, your profile, medicines, reminders and everything the app learns from your recordings are written and kept only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. All of it is produced by software running on the phone; none of it is sent anywhere.

This data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so it is not copied off the phone by a backup either. If you lose the phone or delete the app, it cannot be restored.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording: after you press Record, or start a recording from a Shortcut, Siri, the Action button or the record control. A recording in progress is shown in the Dynamic Island, on the Lock Screen and in the record control.

Face ID

If you turn on "Require Face ID / passcode to open", iOS checks your face or passcode. The app only learns whether the check passed; it never sees or stores your face data.

The language pack download

The app's only network use is downloading its language pack (about 5.5 GB, Google's Gemma model) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), once, when you set up the app or after you remove it. Like any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and ordinary request details; nothing from your recordings or notes is sent. The Telugu speech model is included inside the app.

How long recordings are kept

Raw audio is deleted automatically a set time after it is transcribed: 7 days unless you choose 1 day, 30 days or Forever in Settings → Keep recordings for. A recording still waiting to be transcribed is kept. Transcripts and notes stay until you delete them.

Deleting your data

Delete a single note with Delete note, or use Settings → Delete everything to remove all notes, recordings, your profile, medicines, reminders and everything learned. Settings → Remove to free space deletes the downloaded language pack. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing

Something leaves the phone only when you share it yourself, for example the speed report in Settings. Where it goes then is up to you and the app you share it with.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

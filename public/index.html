What this example does

It searches the public ServiceNow product documentation, picks the pages that match the words in your question, and shows each one with an excerpt and a link. Results are ranked by keyword matching, so a listed page matched the question — reading the source is still how you confirm the answer.

It only covers ServiceNow. Written answers appear only where an answer model is enabled on the deployment, and the tool says which of the two you are looking at before you ask.

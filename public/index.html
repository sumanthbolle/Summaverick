AI tools for everyday work

The problem An AI idea works in a demo, then has no credible route into daily use: no evaluation, no permissions story, and nothing sensible to do when the model is wrong.

Internal tool Public demo

A ServiceNow research agent that shows its working

Who it is for Platform engineers and consultants who need a documented answer about ServiceNow behaviour and cannot afford a confident guess.

The problem A general chatbot answers ServiceNow questions fluently and cites nothing you can check. An earlier version of this agent was worse: it reached only the documentation's table-of-contents pages and reported “citations · verified” over text that did not answer the question.

What we built Two-hop retrieval that picks the publications worth opening and then fetches the topic pages themselves; term weighting so a page sharing only common words cannot rank first; front-matter parsing so metadata never reaches the answer; and four separate checks in place of one verification badge.

What changed The four sample questions on the demo now return the relevant concept or reference page — the CMDB question lands on the CI relationships concept page — and the interface names its own mode: a written answer, matched passages, nothing relevant found, or sources unreachable. A reviewed evaluation covers those four questions plus an off-topic question, a question with no matching documentation, and a run with no model configured.

How delivery works

Three stages, and the same person throughout.

We start from your problem rather than our process, and there is something you can click on early.

01

Understand

A few direct conversations about the work as it happens today, who it breaks for, and what you can safely leave alone.

You get a written scope: what we would build, what we would not, and the open questions.

02

Build

Design and engineering happen together, in the open, with something running that you can use and argue with.

You get working software in your environment, tests, and the reasoning behind the decisions.

03

Hand over

We watch how people actually use it, clear away what slows them down, and leave your team able to change it without us.

You get handover documentation, the support path, and a named owner on your side.

    How the agent works a case.

    The same stages run every time, and the bar above the chat shows where it is.

    1. 01

      Intake

      Reads the order, the item, and what was paid.

    2. 02

      Policy

      Checks the platform's refund rules before asking for anything.

    3. 03

      Draft

      Writes a polite claim, with no personal details in it.

    4. 04

      Negotiate

      Answers the support bot's questions and escalates in tone only when refused.

    5. 05

      Resolve

      Stops at the refund, or hands the case to a person.

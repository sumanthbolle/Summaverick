Research agent

Ask a ServiceNow question.

You will see the work as it happens: how the question was read, where the agent looked, what it declined to trust, and the source behind every line of the answer.

The agent only knows ServiceNow: the Fluent SDK, the product docs, and, when switched on, read-only instance metadata. Questions are rate limited, and anything that reads as a prompt-injection attempt is turned away before it reaches a model. With no model key set, the answer is the draft assembled from the evidence.

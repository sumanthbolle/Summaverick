ServiceNow Buzzword Map: ITSM, CSDM, CMDB, SecOps, IRM, Now Assist and More (Easy Guide)

Beginner · 10 min read · Mar 2026

Why This Buzzword Map Exists

ServiceNow conversations are full of acronyms. This guide gives easy explanations so beginners can connect terms to real enterprise outcomes.

TermEasy MeaningEnterprise Outcome
ITSMRun IT support with standard processesFaster recovery, better service quality
CMDBTrusted technology inventory + relationshipsBetter impact analysis, safer change
CSDMStandard model linking services to techBusiness-impact visibility
SecOpsOperationalize vulnerability/security workFaster remediation, lower risk
IRMConnect risk, controls, complianceAudit readiness, governance clarity
App EngineBuild business apps on platformFaster delivery with governance
Now AssistAI help across workflowsProductivity + consistency gains
ITOMOperations visibility/automationReduced outages and MTTR
HRSDEmployee service delivery workflowsBetter employee support experience
CSMCustomer service operationsBetter case handling and retention

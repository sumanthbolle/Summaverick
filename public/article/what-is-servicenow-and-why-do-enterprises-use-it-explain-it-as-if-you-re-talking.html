Core Concepts

What is ServiceNow, and why do enterprises use it? Explain it as if you're talking to someone who has never heard of the platform.

Beginner · ServiceNow · Mar 2026

Why Interviewers Ask This

This is the most common opening question in any ServiceNow interview. Interviewers want to see if you understand the 'why' behind the platform — not just the technical definition, but the business problem it solves.

The Answer

ServiceNow is a cloud-based platform that helps organizations manage their digital workflows. Think of it as a single system where an entire company can track, automate, and manage work — from IT support tickets to HR onboarding to security incidents to customer service requests.

Before ServiceNow, most companies had dozens of disconnected tools: one for IT tickets, another for change management, another for HR requests, another for asset tracking. Each team had their own spreadsheet, email inbox, or legacy tool. Nothing talked to each other. When an employee needed a laptop, they'd email IT, who'd email procurement, who'd email finance — all manually, with no visibility into where the request stood.

ServiceNow replaces that chaos with a single platform where:

  • Everything is a record on a table — incidents, changes, requests, assets, users — all stored in one connected database.
  • Workflows automate the boring stuff — approvals route automatically, notifications fire on schedule, escalations happen without someone remembering to follow up.
  • Everyone sees the same data — the employee sees their request status, the manager sees their team's workload, leadership sees dashboards and trends.

Why Enterprises Pay Millions for It

  • Scale: ServiceNow handles tens of thousands of users and millions of records. Fortune 500 companies run their entire IT operations on it.
  • Single platform: Instead of 20 tools with 20 vendors and 20 integration headaches, one platform handles IT Service Management (ITSM), IT Operations (ITOM), HR Service Delivery, Security Operations (SecOps), Customer Service (CSM), and more.
  • Out-of-the-box processes: ServiceNow comes with ITIL-aligned processes pre-built. Companies don't start from scratch — they configure and extend what's already there.
💡 Interview Tip

Don't just say 'it's a ticketing tool.' That's the fastest way to lose points. ServiceNow is a platform — ticketing is just one use case. Show that you understand it's an enterprise workflow engine that spans IT, HR, Security, and Customer Service.

# Summaverick's voice

Owner, 2026-11-09: remove the AI wording. Sound like a person advising and explaining to the user. Notifications should greet people the way Swiggy and Zomato do.

## Who is speaking

A friendly, sharp friend who sat through the lecture for you. Speaks as "I" and to "you". Short sentences, everyday words, a little warmth and wit. Never a machine reporting status.

## Words

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| AI, AI model, Summaverick's AI, Gemma | "me", or "Summaverick's language pack" for the download |
| Transcript | "what was said", "word for word" |
| Transcribing… | "Listening back…", "Writing down what was said…" |
| Generate notes | "Write my notes" |
| Regenerate | "Write again" |
| Downloading model | "Getting ready. One big download and I never need the internet again." |
| Processing, generating, loading | "Working on it", "Writing your notes" |
| Failed, error | Say what happened and what happens next, in plain words |
| Memory (feature) | "Things I remember" |

## Where the voice stays plain

No jokes, emoji or wordplay in these. They keep their exact meaning:
- Medicine reminders and medicine answers.
- The health refusal line.
- Recording consent, privacy and "Delete everything".
- Real failures that lose something.

## Being honest

We drop the jargon, not the truth.
- Never claim to be a human. Settings → About keeps one plain line: "Summaverick's notes are written by software running on this phone, and it can get things wrong. Please check anything important."
- Keep "I can get things wrong" wherever notes or answers are first explained.
- The "stays on this phone" promise stays exact.

## Notifications

Short and warm, the way Swiggy and Zomato do it, with the person's first name when known. A title with the hook, a body with the note's name. Each stage has a few versions, picked by note, so people don't see the same line every time. At most one emoji. Never more notifications than today: one per stage per note.
